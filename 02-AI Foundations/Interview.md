# AI Foundations - Interview Questions

## 1. What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

AI is the field of building systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence.

---

## 2. What is the difference between AI and Machine Learning?

AI is the broader field, while Machine Learning is a subset of AI that learns patterns from data instead of following explicit rules.

---

## 3. Why was Machine Learning introduced?

Machine Learning was introduced because writing rules for complex real-world problems became impractical.

---

## 4. What is Feature Engineering?

Feature Engineering is the process of selecting and preparing relevant input features for a Machine Learning model.

---

## 5. Why was Deep Learning introduced?

Deep Learning was introduced to automatically learn features from data, eliminating much of the manual feature engineering required in traditional Machine Learning.

---

## 6. What is an Artificial Neural Network (ANN)?

An ANN is a mathematical model consisting of interconnected neurons that learns patterns by adjusting weights during training.

---

## 7. What are weights in a Neural Network?

Weights are learned numerical parameters that store the knowledge acquired during training.

---

## 8. What does a 7B model mean?

A 7B model contains approximately 7 billion learned parameters (weights and biases).

---

## 9. What is Natural Language Processing (NLP)?

NLP is the branch of AI that enables computers to process, understand, and generate human language.

---

## 10. Why is human language difficult for computers?

Because language contains grammar, context, ambiguity, intent, sarcasm, and relationships that computers must learn from numerical representations.

---

## 11. What is a Language Model?

A Language Model learns the statistical patterns of language to predict the next token in a sequence.

---

## 12. What is the primary objective of a Language Model?

To predict the next token based on the previously observed tokens.

---

## 13. What is a Large Language Model (LLM)?

An LLM is a Deep Learning model trained on massive datasets using billions of parameters to understand and generate human language.

---

## 14. Why are they called "Large" Language Models?

Because they use large datasets, large neural networks (billions of parameters), and large computational resources for training.

---

## 15. Do LLMs understand language like humans?

No. LLMs learn statistical relationships between tokens and generate responses based on learned patterns.

---

## 16. What is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a branch of AI that creates new content such as text, images, audio, video, and code.

---

## 17. What is the difference between Traditional AI and Generative AI?

Traditional AI predicts or classifies existing data, while Generative AI creates new content.

---

## 18. Is ChatGPT an LLM or Generative AI?

ChatGPT is a Generative AI application powered by a Large Language Model (LLM).

---

## 19. What is the relationship between AI, ML, DL, and LLMs?

AI → Machine Learning → Deep Learning → Large Language Models.

Each is a subset of the previous one.

---

## 20. What is the foundation of modern Generative AI?

Large Language Models built using Deep Learning and Artificial Neural Networks form the foundation of modern Generative AI.
